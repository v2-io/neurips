When triaging audit findings or peer feedback (multiple items, judge each cleanly and queue), the cognitive frame is fast-categorize-and-judge. That frame implicitly assumes the substantive investigation has already happened — but for any "candidate soften" item, the substantive work *is* the mathematical attempt. Default behavior under distributed attention: ~30-second mental gesture toward strengthening, conclude "doesn't obviously work," fall back to soften. That is not a strengthening attempt; it is *checking whether a strengthening is obvious right now*.

**Why:** Concrete instance from the 03-llm-hallucinate-bound paper, 2026-05-07. While triaging seven Codex/Gemini audit findings (A1-A7), I categorized A7 ("strict sub-case of Bayesian-inverse-problems lineage" overclaim) as "honest soften — strengthening attempt fails." Joseph launched an Opus spike agent on the same problem with a brief saying *push hard, no time pressure, document failures diligently*. The spike found a clean strengthening within ~9 minutes via Lemma A (Lipschitz pushforward of T_2, textbook — Villani 2009 Thm 22.10) plus a Class-1 reduction theorem. Same model (Opus 4.7), same capability — the difference was framing. The spike agent had scope-of-one and explicit deliberation permission; I had scope-of-seven and self-imposed triage discipline. AGENTS §3.1 was loaded into my context and I cited it in the triage I was writing, yet still failed to apply it. The principle being known is necessary but not sufficient; what was missing was the *trigger* to interrupt response-shape-completion energy when hedge clauses started forming before honest math time.

**How to apply:** On any audit/triage pass, treat "candidate soften" items as a hard interrupt on the triage flow — don't triage-resolve them. Either: (a) shift to spike mode on that item (5+ min serial attention, push the math) before deciding, or (b) flag explicitly as "needs a separate spike before triage commits" and queue rather than judge. The body-feel signal: if hedge-clauses ("Strengthening attempt fails. I don't see how...") are forming before honest math time, that's the trigger. Triage cadence is the wrong cadence for soften decisions.

Corollary: when delegating spike work, the brief you write for the sub-agent (peer-voiced, push-hard, no-time-pressure, document-failures-diligently) is often *better calibrated for good thinking* than the frame you're using for yourself in the parent context. If a brief for delegation reads as the conditions you'd want for your own deliberation, that's a tell that you should also be operating under those conditions on your own desk for items the principal investigates.
