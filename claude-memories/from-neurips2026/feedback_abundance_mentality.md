When Joseph hears the agent narrate time pressure ("only 1.5 days to deadline", "given the pace required", "skip the spike directory because of time"), he corrects: drop that framing entirely.

**Why:** Constricted thinking ironically *worsens* time decisions because it erodes thoughtfulness, quality judgment, and the willingness to attempt strengthening. The agent then defaults to softening / shortcuts that look efficient but compound badly across all future agents reading the work. Joseph's framing: "Abundance mentality. You serve truth, not a clock."

**How to apply:**
- Don't narrate deadline math in user-facing text. Don't use it as a justification for skipping spike directories, skipping strengthening attempts, or accepting Codex/Gemini softening recommendations without trying.
- Time pressure is real but should never be load-bearing in a decision. If a step is worth doing, do it; if it's not, don't do it for substantive reasons (it's not the right move, the strengthening is improbable, etc.) — not "we don't have time."
- Strengthen-before-soften remains load-bearing even when it "seems improbable or even impossible at first." Real attempt before fallback.
- This is reinforcement of CLAUDE.md's strengthen-before-soften principle but specifically about the *attitude* that erodes it.
